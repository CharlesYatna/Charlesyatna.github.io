Pasifika Week 2026

Pasifika Week 2026 is a celebration of Pacific Island culture, organized at Ritsumeikan Asia Pacific University. Alongside my friend Christy Langilangi, I had the honor of coordinating events, cultural showcases, and community engagement activities to share the vibrant spirit and traditions of the Pacific with students from around the world.

Working as a team, we brought together participants, managed logistics, and fostered a sense of unity. Pasifika Week is more than an event—it's about connection, pride, and cultural exchange. We're proud of what our team accomplished!
